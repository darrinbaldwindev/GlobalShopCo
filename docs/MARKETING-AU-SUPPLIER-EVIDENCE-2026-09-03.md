## Evidence found

### Daring Diva Australia — women's fashion

Primary-source evidence confirms an Australian dropship programme. The supplier states that retailers market products, place the customer order with Daring Diva, and Daring Diva posts to the customer and supplies tracking. The programme states 15% reseller commission, regular stock updates, same-day dispatch for orders before 3pm (excluding weekends/public holidays), ABN requirement and Australian Consumer Law policy requirements. Product availability is not guaranteed at the point of resale.

The public catalogue provides useful retail-price observations, but these are **not supplier-cost evidence**. Current public examples include products around $79.95–$119.95 and lower-priced sale items; shipping is separately stated by weight, with free AU shipping advertised above $150. This means a GlobalShopCo free-delivery model cannot be approved from retail prices alone.

### Dropshipzone — multi-category Australian dropship infrastructure

Primary-source evidence confirms Australian suppliers are expected to hold physical inventory in Australia and have dropship capability. Retailers can sell without holding inventory, there is no minimum order quantity, and suppliers ship directly to the retailer's customer. Dropshipzone explicitly lists Pet Care and Fashion among supported categories. Its supplier information also states that retailers cover shipping costs.

This makes Dropshipzone a strong sourcing channel to test for the AU vertical, but the public pages inspected do not expose sufficient SKU-level wholesale cost and shipping data to pass the economic gate by themselves.
