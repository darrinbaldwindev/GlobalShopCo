## Priority suppliers

### Southern Pet Supplies / Southern Pet Dropship

Primary-source evidence confirms direct-to-customer dropshipping from an Australian warehouse. The supplier states that customers receive no supplier pricing information, the retailer charges its own normal retail price, and goods are sent directly to the customer's address. Published metro South Australia shipping starts from $6.09, with an Australia-wide rate table available. The supplier states no signup/account fees, no minimum spend and no handling or transaction fees; displayed supplier prices exclude GST.

**Why it matters:** this is unusually strong evidence for a free-delivery model because delivery economics are explicitly surfaced. The remaining gate is obtaining actual SKU cost/retail-price data and calculating contribution by destination.

### Pet Now

Primary-source evidence confirms an Australian pet distributor offering dropship service and multi-warehouse fulfilment across QLD, NSW and VIC. Its range includes Petsbuddy, PetPure, Kojima and ABC Pet Group. Account application is required.

**Why it matters:** geographic warehouse coverage could support faster AU fulfilment, but public pages do not expose sufficient SKU economics to approve products.

### Petgo Pet Supplies

Primary-source evidence confirms direct-to-customer dropshipping. The supplier charges a processing fee of 5% for orders up to $150 and 2% for orders above $150, plus actual postage based on Australia Post/Border Express/other courier rates. Tracking is provided after shipment.

**Why it matters:** the processing fee materially affects contribution, so low-AOV products may be unsuitable for GlobalShopCo's free-delivery promise.
