# GlobalShopCo — AU Vertical SKU Gate — 2026-09-03

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** RESEARCH SPECIFICATION  
**Branch:** `agent/overseer/initial-project-timeline`

## Objective

Turn the category decision into a product-level evidence test. No final AU vertical is selected until actual products survive this gate.

## Required SKU record

For each candidate SKU capture:

- merchant/supplier and source;
- product/variant identity;
- Australian availability;
- wholesale or dropship eligibility;
- supplier/product cost;
- customer price;
- domestic delivery cost;
- free-delivery treatment;
- payment/transaction cost assumption;
- expected contribution per order;
- returns/refund conditions;
- stock/fulfilment evidence;
- product claims requiring verification;
- date checked.

## Candidate comparison

Score each vertical on evidence quality, contribution economics, fulfilment simplicity, returns burden, customer intent, comparison/content potential and expansion potential.

Do not use category size or nominal margin as a substitute for SKU economics.

## Initial test set

Compare 5–10 SKUs in each of the leading candidate verticals, beginning with women's fashion/boho and pet products. If a candidate cannot produce enough economically viable, fulfilment-verifiable SKUs, it fails the first-launch gate regardless of category popularity.

## Free-delivery calculation

Contribution must be assessed after product cost, expected delivery cost and applicable transaction costs. A low-price product should not be approved simply because gross margin percentage appears attractive.

## Decision states

RESEARCHED → EVIDENCE COMPLETE → ECONOMICALLY VIABLE → FIRST-VERTICAL CANDIDATE → APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION
