### Free-delivery stress test

At a hypothetical $49.95 retail price, a $3.00 supplier cost leaves substantial gross-margin headroom before freight, but this is NOT a launch margin because outbound freight is unknown and the retail price is not yet validated against an exact comparable. The low supplier costs make these particularly attractive for multi-item baskets, where freight can be amortised across several products.

### K&A risks

- Freight is quoted after enquiry; cannot yet calculate landed cost.
- Wholesale MOQs mean the supplier is better suited to stocked inventory than individual dropship unless K&A confirms fulfilment capability.
- Exact retail competitors and market prices still need product-level matching.
- Claims such as BPA-free/Made in Australia should remain CLAIMED until documentation is obtained.
