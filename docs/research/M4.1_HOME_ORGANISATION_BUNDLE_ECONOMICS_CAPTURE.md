It also confirms that Sydney Metro orders over $200 ex GST receive free direct delivery from CWS's in-house fleet; orders below that threshold are charged delivery and sent with a partnered courier.

CWS's current contact/FAQ page additionally states that members of the public can order directly without an ABN, that there is no strict minimum order, and that delivery uses CWS's in-house team plus trusted partners including AusPost and Aramex. ## Independent retail benchmark search — 2026-09-04

A fresh Australian web search was performed for an exact or sufficiently comparable 3L storage basket, prioritising matching dimensions/capacity and a consumer-facing seller independent of CWS. The search did **not** establish a sufficiently comparable exact match.

Results included 3L glass storage jars and pantry containers at Australian retailers, but these are materially different products/materials and are therefore rejected as price benchmarks for SG118655. Other storage-basket results were materially different sizes/materials or marketplace listings without enough evidence of comparability. No retail price has been promoted into the economics table from these results.

Examples of rejected comparisons:
- Lemon & Lime 3L Cosmo glass jar: 15 × 15 × 23cm; different product/material and therefore not a valid SG118655 benchmark.
- Lemon & Lime 3L Keep Fresh vegetable storer: 23 × 16.5 × 10.5cm; different product/material/use and therefore not a valid SG118655 benchmark.
- Generic cotton-rope storage baskets: different materials and dimensions; insufficient comparability.

The absence of a defensible benchmark is itself recorded as an evidence result. Do not use these rejected products to manufacture a margin assumption.

## Calculation rules

For each scenario:

`Gross sales = units × consumer price per unit`

`Variable cost = supplier goods cost + supplier freight + fulfilment fee + payment/platform fees + other variable costs`

`Contribution before fixed costs = Gross sales − Variable cost`

`Contribution margin % = Contribution before fixed costs ÷ Gross sales`

If GST applies to both revenue and costs, keep GST treatment consistent rather than mixing GST-inclusive retail with GST-exclusive supplier inputs.

## Decision gate

Do not promote the candidate from **HOLD** unless all of the following are evidenced for the scenario being proposed:

1. realistic consumer price from an exact or sufficiently comparable Australian offer;
2. destination-specific freight or a defensible contracted/quoted freight rule;
3. confirmed supplier fulfilment model, including whether blind/dropship fulfilment is supported;
4. returns/warranty treatment;
5. positive contribution after all evidenced variable costs;
6. no material compliance/trust issue identified for the proposed listing.

A positive spreadsheet result using `TBD`, unsupported assumptions, supplier-channel prices, or benchmark retail prices that are not sufficiently comparable is **not** launch evidence.
