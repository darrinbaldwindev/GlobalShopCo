## Fresh public-source verification — 2026-09-04

The current CWS product page confirms the $3.50 unit price, SKU SG118655, 23 × 17 × 8cm dimensions, individual-sale basis, random colour selection, and the published transit bands above. It also confirms that Sydney Metro orders over $200 ex GST receive free direct delivery from CWS's in-house fleet; orders below that threshold are charged delivery and sent with a partnered courier.

CWS's current contact/FAQ page additionally states that members of the public can order directly without an ABN, that there is no strict minimum order, and that delivery uses CWS's in-house team plus trusted partners including AusPost and Aramex. These facts support the feasibility of small-order purchasing from the supplier, but they do **not** establish that CWS will blind-ship on GlobalShopCo's behalf.

**Evidence boundary:** the public pages do **not** expose the candidate's actual freight charge to a customer destination, and they do not establish dropship/blind-fulfilment support. Therefore neither item is promoted from UNKNOWN to EVIDENCED. Because CWS sells to the general public as well as businesses, any CWS consumer-facing price should also be treated as a supplier-channel reference, not automatically as an independent retail-market comparable.

## Calculation rules

For each scenario:

`Gross sales = units × consumer price per unit`

`Variable cost = supplier goods cost + supplier freight + fulfilment fee + payment/platform fees + other variable costs`

`Contribution before fixed costs = Gross sales − Variable cost`

`Contribution margin % = Contribution before fixed costs ÷ Gross sales`

If GST applies to both revenue and costs, keep GST treatment consistent rather than mixing GST-inclusive retail with GST-exclusive supplier inputs.

## Decision gate

Do not promote the candidate from **HOLD** unless all of the following are evidenced for the scenario being proposed:

1. realistic consumer price from an exact or sufficiently comparable Australian offer;
2. destination-specific freight or a defensible contracted/quoted freight rule;
3. confirmed supplier fulfilment model, including whether blind/dropship fulfilment is supported;
4. returns/warranty treatment;
5. positive contribution after all evidenced variable costs;
6. no material compliance/trust issue identified for the proposed listing.

A positive spreadsheet result using `TBD`, unsupported assumptions, supplier-channel prices, or benchmark retail prices that are not sufficiently comparable is **not** launch evidence.
