## Calculation rules

For each scenario:

`Gross sales = units × consumer price per unit`

`Variable cost = supplier goods cost + supplier freight + fulfilment fee + payment/platform fees + other variable costs`

`Contribution before fixed costs = Gross sales − Variable cost`

`Contribution margin % = Contribution before fixed costs ÷ Gross sales`

If GST applies to both revenue and costs, keep GST treatment consistent rather than mixing GST-inclusive retail with GST-exclusive supplier inputs.

## Decision gate

Do not promote the candidate from **HOLD** unless all of the following are evidenced for the scenario being proposed:

1. realistic consumer price from an exact or sufficiently comparable Australian offer;
2. destination-specific freight or a defensible contracted/quoted freight rule;
3. confirmed supplier fulfilment model, including whether blind/dropship fulfilment is supported;
4. returns/warranty treatment;
5. positive contribution after all evidenced variable costs;
6. no material compliance/trust issue identified for the proposed listing.

A positive spreadsheet result using `TBD`, unsupported assumptions, or benchmark retail prices is **not** launch evidence.
