## Free-delivery assessment

No candidate can currently be marked free-delivery viable because supplier wholesale cost and candidate-level shipping cost are not publicly verified. The products should therefore remain **RESEARCH / PRICE-REQUEST** rather than launch-ready.

The strongest commercial pattern is small-to-medium, lightweight organisers that can be bundled into larger baskets. Large carts, racks and wheeled products are materially weaker until freight is proven.
