## Ranked Home Organisation shortlist

### 1. K&A H9016 — 6L plastic storage box with removable dividers
**Status: AMBER / PROMISING MULTIPACK / HOLD**

- Exact SKU: H9016.
- Dimensions: 39.5 × 25.7 × 9.5 cm.
- MOQ: 10.
- Public wholesale: A$7.59 + GST each for 10–100 units; freight excluded.
- Current same-style Australian multipack benchmarks imply roughly A$13.33–A$14.99 retail per unit before delivery differences.
- Gross spread before freight/payment/packaging/returns is only about A$4.98–A$6.64 per unit depending on comparison used.
- Single-unit free delivery therefore fails closed.
- A six-pack remains a test hypothesis because one parcel may amortise freight across multiple units.

**Blocking evidence:** supplier freight, carton/packed weight, dropship/blind-ship support, stock, payment cost, packaging, return allowance.

## Commercial rule

GlobalShopCo offers free delivery. Therefore wholesale-to-retail spread is not enough. A candidate only progresses when contribution remains acceptable after landed goods cost, customer freight, payment cost, packaging, returns/damage allowance and applicable tax treatment.

Do not infer zero freight from missing freight. Do not use a supplier's bulk-inbound free-delivery threshold as proof of free customer delivery.
