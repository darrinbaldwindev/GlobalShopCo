## Evidence state

### Verified / strong source evidence
- Concrete product names and dimensions from supplier catalogues.
- Several exact supplier SKU/product codes.
- Public wholesale prices for K&A candidates.
- Public wholesale prices and shipping policy for reviewed DPW candidates.
- Australian retail benchmark evidence for selected DPW product.

### Claimed / requires independent confirmation
- Supplier fulfilment performance.
- Dropship availability.
- Current stock beyond what is displayed publicly.
- Candidate-level delivery cost for several suppliers.

### Unknown / blocking launch decisions
- Landed cost for most candidates.
- Supplier-specific dropship terms.
- Candidate-level freight for K&A and United Living.
- Final competitive price for many exact SKUs.
- Product-level returns/warranty treatment for several suppliers.

## Commercial rule

GlobalShopCo offers free delivery. Therefore **low wholesale cost alone is insufficient**. Priority should go to products that are small/light, have sufficient selling-price headroom, can be bundled into $50+ baskets, and have low damage/return risk.
