## Newly established supplier terms

CWS publishes a fault/incorrect-item return process requiring assessment and
evidence. It says it covers reasonable return freight for confirmed faulty or
incorrect goods, offers no change-of-mind returns, and typically resolves claims
2–10 business days after assessment. Product-specific warranty duration and
reseller/customer handling arrangements remain UNKNOWN. This records supplier
terms, not a legal assessment. [CWS refund policy](https://completewholesalesuppliers.com.au/policies/refund-policy).

Smart-Link's delivery page states charges depend on size, weight, destination
and shipping method, confirmed at checkout or before dispatch. It also contains
an older pallet-rate section effective June 2021 and a purchasing guide specifying
a $1,500 + GST first order and possible additional freight invoicing. Current
applicability requires confirmation; the old pallet rates are not parcel quotes.
This substantially weakens its suitability for a minimal-stock dropship start.
[Smart-Link delivery policy](https://www.smartlinkhome.com.au/delivery-policy/).

Smart-Link's posted returns terms request fault notification within seven days,
prior return authorization and photographs; approved faulty/incorrect returns
have reasonable return freight arranged or reimbursed. It excludes change-of-mind
returns. These are supplier statements, not GlobalShopCo consumer-policy wording.
[Smart-Link returns policy](https://www.smartlinkhome.com.au/returns-policy/).
