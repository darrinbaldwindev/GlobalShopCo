## BLOCKERS

- Several suppliers do not publish candidate-level outbound freight publicly.
- Smart-Link wholesale pricing is login-gated.
- United Living wholesale pricing/freight is not public in the reviewed catalogue.
- DPW reviewed storage candidates showed backorder/unavailable status.

### Likely
- Free-delivery viability will be strongest for small/light products bundled into higher-value baskets rather than bulky standalone storage.

### Unknown
- Dropship capability for all reviewed suppliers unless explicitly confirmed.
- Final landed costs and destination-specific freight for most candidates.
- Final market selling price for many exact SKUs.
- Product-level warranty/returns terms for several suppliers.
