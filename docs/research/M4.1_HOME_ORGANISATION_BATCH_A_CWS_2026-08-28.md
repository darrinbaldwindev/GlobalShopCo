### Commercial implication

The new evidence **does not support an individual high-margin retail assumption yet**. Public retail evidence shows 3L/near-3L storage products can sell at low per-unit equivalents when sold in multipacks. CWS's $3.50 wholesale cost therefore needs either:

- a materially higher single-unit consumer price justified by design/quality/utility, **or**
- bundle/multi-unit merchandising where the order-level freight is spread across several baskets and/or adjacent products.

Do **not** treat the BIG W pack-equivalent prices as recommended GlobalShopCo selling prices; pack size, brand, dimensions and fulfilment economics differ.

## Assessment

This remains a potentially useful **basket-builder**, but not a launch-ready hero SKU. The compact dimensions are favourable for freight compared with bulky storage, and the supplier's 2–4 business-day published transit window for QLD/VIC is commercially useful. However, the $200 ex-GST free-delivery threshold means GlobalShopCo cannot model free delivery from unit cost alone. Dropship capability, actual freight, returns/warranty terms and destination-specific landed cost remain unknown.

The 2026-09-04 retail check also lowers confidence in using a simple $9–$10 retail assumption without further evidence. A bundle-led test is the stronger hypothesis.
