# GlobalShopCo — AU Pet Vertical Priority Update — 2026-09-03

**Status:** PRIORITY CANDIDATE / ECONOMICS NOT YET VERIFIED

## Why pet has moved up

Primary-source supplier research now shows multiple Australian dropship pathways and unusually useful supplier guidance on where margin exists.

Southern Pet Supplies states that pet accessories are where online pet stores make the bulk of profit, specifically identifying toys, walking accessories and bedding. It states 50–90% markup is achievable on GiGwi pet toys and 50–90% profit on Snooza bedding, while warning that major supermarket brands and cheap entry-level beds are difficult to compete on. It also warns dog clothing can generate 40–100% margins but has materially higher fit-return risk.

Southern Pet additionally provides Australia-wide dropship shipping rates, publishes product weights/cubic data and updates stock levels every five minutes. Dropship orders have no minimum order, while change-of-mind returns incur a 10% restocking fee and food/medicine/supplements have tighter return rules.

## Commercial interpretation

This is stronger evidence for a curated **pet accessories** vertical than for a generic pet-products store. The likely first test should prioritise lightweight, non-regulated accessories with clear consumer utility and enough selling price to absorb free delivery.

Avoid initially:

- commodity supermarket pet food;
- regulated/health-sensitive products;
- bulky/fragile products;
- low-price SKUs where free delivery destroys contribution;
- dog clothing until return economics are modelled.

## Required next test

Build a 5–10 SKU evidence table from Southern Pet and/or other Australian suppliers containing actual supplier cost, current retail market price, product weight, shipping cost, free-delivery contribution, stock status and returns treatment.

No SKU or vertical is approved from markup statements alone.
