The prior one-family/one-SKU research cadence produced too little verified listing inventory. Owner authorised a throughput change: build a materially larger qualified pipeline while preserving the existing evidence and safety gates.

## New batch doctrine
From this batch onward:
- screen 10–25 exact SKUs/candidates per batch where evidence permits;
- use broad screening first, then deep verification only for the strongest candidates;
- target 150–200 screened candidates to yield ~100 commercially promising products and progressively verify them to GREEN;
- never lower Supplier, Freight, Economics, Quality, Compliance or Marketplace Fit gates;
- never call a SKU listing-ready while authenticated cost, freight, stock or resale permission remains unresolved;
- Shopify remains canonical; Marketplace Connect remains intended downstream connector;
- no autonomous publication, supplier contact, purchase, credentials, spend, merge or deployment.

## Supplier-path findings
### DropshipOnly
Public supplier material states it supports wholesale dropshipping in Australia, has 50,000+ SKUs, no minimum quantity, dispatches within 24 hours and ships Australia-wide, including car hooks, magnetic phone mounts and trunk organisers. This is a high-value catalogue for authenticated screening, but exact wholesale prices, marketplace permission and SKU-level stock/freight remain UNKNOWN.

### Storage Organisers
Public FAQ accepts trade/wholesale enquiries. A Syncee supplier record identifies Storage Organisers as an Australian supplier and exposes catalogue families while wholesale prices remain gated. This is a promising source for compact differentiated home organisation but not yet authenticated for GlobalShopCo.

### Smart Link Homewares
Public material describes Australian-owned wholesale supply, locally stocked inventory, Australia-wide delivery and suitability for online sellers. Exact wholesale pricing is login-gated. Candidate admission remains HOLD pending exact SKU economics and dropship/marketplace terms.

### Pet Accessories Australia
Public FAQ explicitly says it is wholesale-only but **does not support dropshipping**. Therefore it is not suitable for the current zero-inventory first-wave model despite its brand depth. Do not spend verification capacity there unless the inventory strategy changes.
