# GlobalShopCo — Pet Vertical GTM Gate — 2026-09-04

## Decision position
Pet products are now a **high-priority first-vertical candidate**, but remain **NOT APPROVED FOR LAUNCH** until supplier/SKU economics are evidenced.

## Marketing implications

### VERIFIED MARKET SIGNALS
- Pet has substantial and growing Australian online spend.
- Online pet shopping is not a niche-only channel; more than one-third of category spend was online in 2025.
- Delivery certainty and speed are commercially relevant.
- Value sensitivity matters.
- Repeat-purchase and bundle opportunities are relevant to the category.

### INFERENCE / RECOMMENDATION
For an initial GlobalShopCo pet range, favour products that are:
- non-regulated and low-risk;
- simple to explain and compare;
- durable enough for parcel delivery;
- capable of healthy contribution after freight/free delivery/payment costs;
- suitable for bundles or repeat purchasing where economics support it;
- supported by reliable stock and tracking;
- backed by clear product facts and returns information.

### UNKNOWN
- GlobalShopCo conversion rate.
- Customer acquisition cost.
- SKU-level contribution margin.
- Supplier reliability in actual operation.
- Repeat purchase rate.
- Whether AI discovery produces material traffic or revenue.

## Launch gate
A pet vertical can move from candidate to **FIRST-VERTICAL CANDIDATE / APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION** only after:

1. 5–10 actual SKUs are selected.
2. Supplier eligibility and dropship terms are authenticated.
3. Supplier cost and freight are authenticated.
4. Stock/feed/tracking behaviour is evidenced.
5. Customer price is established.
6. Free-delivery cost is modelled.
7. Payment/transaction costs are modelled.
8. Returns/refunds/damage liability is understood.
9. Contribution is positive under conservative assumptions.
10. Product/customer-trust and applicable compliance checks pass.

## Important constraint
Market size must not be substituted for unit economics. The Australia Post evidence strengthens **demand confidence**, not **profitability confidence**.
