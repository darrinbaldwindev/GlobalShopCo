# Marketing Role & Next Actions

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer
**Date:** 2026-09-03

## Marketing requirements

- Define the highest-value Australian-first customer/product proposition.
- Position GlobalShopCo around trusted product discovery, free-delivery economics and niche/category usefulness without unsupported claims.
- Validate supplier, dropship, margin, shipping and fulfilment propositions before publication.
- Define SEO/AEO category architecture and high-intent landing pages.
- Build product/category comparison and decision-support messaging.
- Establish conversion paths from discovery to product selection to Shopify checkout.
- Maintain evidence-backed claims and affiliate/commercial disclosure.
- Test acquisition channels, offers and merchandising using measured conversion and contribution economics.

## Immediate marketing action

Prioritise a small set of high-intent Australian vertical categories and prove the discovery → comparison → Shopify checkout journey before expanding catalogue breadth. Free delivery must be treated as an economic constraint, not merely promotional copy.

## Boundary
Marketing does not alter Shopify authority, checkout, credentials or production infrastructure. Implementation remains with the project/technical owner.
