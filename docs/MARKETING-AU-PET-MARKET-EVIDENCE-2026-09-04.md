# GlobalShopCo — AU Pet Market Evidence — 2026-09-04

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** VERIFIED MARKET CONTEXT / NOT SKU ECONOMICS  
**Purpose:** Record current Australian pet-market evidence relevant to the first-vertical decision without confusing market demand with product-level viability.

## Verified market signals

Animal Medicines Australia’s 2025 *Pets in Australia* report states that 73% of Australian households (about 7.7 million) have a pet and estimates 31.6 million pets nationally. Dogs are present in 49% of households and cats in 34%.

The same 2025 report estimates annual Australian spending of $21.3 billion on pets, including approximately $1.4 billion on products/accessories, $1.0 billion on pet insurance, and $1.0 billion on flea/worming/tick treatments. Food accounts for $9.8 billion.

For products and accessories, the 2025 survey reports that 24% of pet owners primarily obtain them from pet-specialty stores in person, 22% from supermarkets, 16% from general retail stores, and 14% from pet-specialty stores online. Online subscriptions account for 3%.

The 2025 report also says ongoing cost is the leading barrier among people who would like another pet, at 26%, indicating that value and price sensitivity should remain central to merchandising and acquisition strategy.

## Marketing interpretation

1. **Pet is commercially credible as a market category.** The addressable owner base and annual category spending are substantial.
2. **Online is a real but minority primary channel.** The 14% online-specialty-store figure supports an online proposition but does not prove that a new entrant can acquire customers profitably.
3. **Value positioning matters.** Cost pressure is explicit in the current survey; free delivery can be a useful proposition only if SKU economics support it.
4. **Accessories remain more suitable for early GlobalShopCo testing than regulated healthcare products.** Healthcare products carry additional compliance and trust requirements and should not be treated as interchangeable with ordinary accessories.
5. **Demand evidence does not approve a supplier or SKU.** Supplier cost, freight, stock, returns, compliance, customer price and contribution after free delivery remain the commercial gate.
