# GlobalShopCo — Agentic Commerce Discoverability Evidence — 2026-09-04

## Purpose
Capture a current Australian market signal relevant to GlobalShopCo's future product discovery, structured catalogue, trust and transaction strategy.

## Primary evidence
PwC Australia, **Agentic commerce explained: What Australian retailers need to know**, published 17 August 2026.

PwC describes agentic commerce as AI agents increasingly researching products, evaluating options and potentially completing purchases on behalf of customers. It identifies a shift from human-led search/browse behaviour toward agents evaluating structured information and recommends retailers become **discoverable, trustworthy, structured and transactable**.

PwC also identifies three emerging interaction models: branded agents, personal shopping agents and multi-agent autonomy. It specifically describes third-party agents such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini as a future interface through which retailers may need to be discovered and transacted with.

## Marketing interpretation

### VERIFIED
- Agentic commerce is an active 2026 Australian retail topic documented by a major professional-services firm.
- PwC explicitly identifies machine-readable/structured product information, trusted signals and transactable pathways as important preparation areas.
- The article states that Australian retailers are still in the early stages of agentic adoption.

### INFERENCE / RECOMMENDATION
GlobalShopCo should design its catalogue and content so that product information can eventually serve both humans and machine/AI discovery systems. This reinforces existing requirements for clean product data, evidence, availability, pricing, delivery, returns and transparent commercial relationships.

### UNKNOWN
- The proportion of GlobalShopCo traffic or revenue that could come from AI agents.
- Which AI shopping agents will materially drive Australian commerce.
- Whether GlobalShopCo's eventual Shopify/WordPress implementation will be directly transactable by third-party agents.
- Incremental conversion, CAC or revenue attributable to agentic discoverability.

## Practical marketing requirements

1. Keep product facts structured and consistent: title, variant, price, availability, delivery, returns, material/specification and suitability.
2. Preserve provenance and freshness for important commercial claims.
3. Avoid content designed only for keyword ranking; useful comparison and decision information should remain understandable to people and extractable by machines.
4. Treat AI-agent discoverability as an additional future acquisition/distribution requirement, not a current traffic or revenue assumption.
5. Do not claim GlobalShopCo is agent-ready until the actual implementation and transaction path are tested.

## Gate
This evidence strengthens a **future marketing/design requirement**. It does **not** approve a first vertical, supplier, SKU, affiliate relationship, revenue forecast or production AI-commerce integration.

## Source
PwC Australia, 17 August 2026: “Agentic commerce explained: What Australian retailers need to know.”
