## Purpose

Identify supplier lanes where public first-party terms are strong enough to materially narrow acquisition economics before any account creation or supplier contact.

## Eleganter Australia — materially improved

First-party dropship terms state:
- no minimum order requirement for individual dropship orders;
- no additional dropship handling fee;
- handling is built into shipping;
- costs are the item price as listed plus freight and GST;
- orders are generally shipped within 2 working days unless separately agreed;
- dropship courier options include Australia Post and Fastway/Aramex;
- exact dropship freight rates are not published and must be obtained through staff/account/checkout.

This is stronger than a generic `partner price unknown` supplier state. It means the Eleganter item-price field is directly relevant to dropship screening, but exact live account/checkout price and freight still require verification because public catalogue pages can disagree or be stale.

### Immediate consequence
`CA3044` Koala Dream Silicone Castle Pour & Play Bath Set becomes the highest-value Baby account-data unlock because:
- exact product identity is independently verified;
- public market price is around A$29.75–A$35;
- Eleganter indexed item price conflicts between A$12.95 and A$25.95;
- exact dropship freight is the remaining decisive cost input.

If the live item price is near A$12.95, deeper qualification is justified immediately. If near A$25.95, reject as a standalone free-delivery SKU.

## 3 Little Crowns — promising but still ambiguous

First-party evidence confirms:
- wholesale prices are fixed for stockists;
- RRP discipline applies;
- standard wholesale orders have A$500 minimum unless agreed otherwise;
- freight is calculated by weight/volume;
- a public collection titled `DROPSHIP 40%` exists.

However, public evidence does not explicitly define what `40%` means operationally for an approved GlobalShopCo dropship order, nor confirm dropship freight treatment. Do not convert the collection title into a 40%-off acquisition price without authenticated programme terms.

## Ozdingo — operationally attractive, acquisition price still gated

First-party dropship pages confirm:
- Australian operation/stock;
- free Australian shipping;
- daily dispatch target;
- 30-day money-back policy;
- Ozdingo Connect subscription is publicly A$10/month for up to 10 SKUs.

Wholesale pricing remains enquiry/account-driven. Public storefront prices are not safe to treat as dropship acquisition cost.

## Essential Dog — formal dropship lane, price gated

First-party evidence confirms:
- dropshipping is available through its website;
- no dropship subscription fee;
- account setup is required;
- Syncee and DropshipZone are also supported partner routes.

Exact acquisition pricing and shipping economics remain account-gated.

## Decision

Public research can now rank supplier lanes by `distance to a commercial decision`:
1. **Eleganter** — closest; exact item price is meaningful, freight/account confirmation remains.
2. **3 Little Crowns** — potentially strong differentiated margin, but programme discount semantics/freight need authentication.
3. **Ozdingo** — strong fulfilment/free-shipping model, acquisition cost gated.
4. **Essential Dog** — useful Pet differentiation, acquisition cost gated.

Do not spend further cycles rediscovering these programme facts. Next progress requires exact account/checkout economics or independent market validation of candidate SKUs.
