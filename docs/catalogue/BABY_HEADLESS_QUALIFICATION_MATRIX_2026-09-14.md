## Category interpretation

### Strongest current Baby lanes
1. **Bath & Care / feeding textiles** — low complexity, easy to ship, strong bundle potential.
2. **Compact tested baby toys / rattles / teethers** — promising only when exact product-specific safety scope and partner economics are available.
3. **Feeding accessories** — potentially useful, but branded commodity items require exact competitor checks because retail price compression is likely.
4. **Nursery organisation / portable care** — attractive where compact; bulky furniture is a later wave.

### First-wave exclusions/defer
- powered baby monitors;
- sleep-positioning or sleep-claim products;
- heavy nursery furniture where freight is not locked;
- any candidate whose safety/compliance claim depends only on generic supplier copy rather than product-specific evidence;
- public retail-price products where partner acquisition price is not authenticated.
