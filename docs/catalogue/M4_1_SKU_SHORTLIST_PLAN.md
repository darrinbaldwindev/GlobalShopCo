# M4.1 — SKU Shortlist Plan

**Date:** 2026-08-26
**Status:** Active research plan — no SKU is launch-approved

## Objective

Move from category research to a small, evidence-backed initial SKU shortlist that can eventually be loaded into the Shopify master catalogue.

## Validation order

### Batch A — Home organisation
Prioritise compact products such as drawer/wardrobe organisers, cable/storage organisers, bathroom storage and practical kitchen organisation.

### Batch B — Mobile/computer accessories
Prioritise lightweight phone/computer accessories with low warranty complexity: cases, screen protection, cable management, stands and selected charging accessories where Australian compliance is clear.

### Batch C — Pet accessories
Prioritise compact grooming, walking, feeding, toy and travel accessories. Exclude medicines, pesticides/parasiticides, food and perishables from the initial batch.

### Batch D — Beauty accessories
Prioritise non-therapeutic accessories and tools. Avoid products with unverified therapeutic/medical claims.

### Batch E — Baby/Kids
Only progress products where Australian safety/compliance documentation is sufficiently clear.

## Evidence sources

Market/category demand should use current Australian evidence, with Australia Post 2026 as the baseline. Supplier evidence should come from the supplier/platform itself wherever possible. A supplier platform is not proof that every SKU is suitable.

## Scoring model

Score each candidate 0–5 for:

- Demand
- Supplier reliability/evidence
- Landed-margin potential
- Free-delivery fit
- Delivery speed/coverage
- Return/warranty risk (reverse score)
- Compliance risk (reverse score)
- Cross-sell potential
- Vertical reuse

**Maximum:** 45 points.

### Decision bands

- **38–45:** strong candidate for launch validation
- **32–37:** candidate; validate missing evidence
- **25–31:** hold/research further
- **<25:** reject/deprioritise

No score overrides missing mandatory evidence. A candidate without supplier, cost, delivery and applicable compliance evidence remains unverified.

## Required output per SKU

Product name | SKU/model | Supplier | supplier status | product cost | shipping | landed cost | proposed sell price | comparable market price | margin after free delivery | stock | delivery time | returns/warranty | compliance | verticals | score | decision.

## Commercial rule

Do not optimise for catalogue size. Optimise for a compact first assortment that can support the free-delivery proposition and provide useful cross-sell opportunities.

## Current evidence note

Australia Post reports $82.6B Australian online spend in 2025, a $96 average basket and strong category spend across Home & Garden, Consumer Electronics, Fashion & Apparel and other categories. Its 2026 report also notes that shoppers are increasingly value-focused and that free-shipping thresholds/cross-sell can be used to encourage larger baskets. Supplier research identifies Dropshipzone as a cross-category candidate platform, but individual SKU validation remains mandatory.

**Next action:** collect actual SKU-level evidence for Batches A–C before expanding the shortlist.
