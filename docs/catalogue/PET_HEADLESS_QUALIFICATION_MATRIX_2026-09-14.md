## Supplier lane assessment

### Southern Pet Supplies — PRIMARY EVIDENCE-RICH PET LANE

Current first-party evidence establishes:
- formal dropship program;
- Australia-wide direct-to-customer fulfilment;
- no signup/account/handling/transaction fees;
- published freight tables;
- exact product catalogue with SKU/stock/weight visibility for researched candidates;
- displayed trade prices require authorised account/login.

Southern Pet's marketplace restriction applies to Amazon/eBay/Kogan/other marketplaces without written approval. Do not automatically extend that restriction to the ordinary owned Shopify/headless channel; nevertheless, owned-site dropship/customer standards must be respected and final account terms must be checked before sale.

Main blocker: authenticated trade buy cost.

### Dropshipzone — HIGH-BREADTH STRUCTURED LANE

Current first-party evidence establishes:
- free retailer signup;
- active ABN/ACN plus store required;
- no wholesale-order or inventory-holding requirement for ordinary dropship use;
- product costs are GST-inclusive;
- AU suppliers are expected to hold Australian inventory and dropship capability;
- Shopify app/feed support and stock sync for many products;
- broad Pet Care taxonomy including dog beds, bowls/feeders, carriers, collars/leads/harnesses, grooming, playpens/gates, steps/ramps and toys.

Important limitation: price, stock and shipping detail is account-gated in the catalogue. No Dropshipzone candidate should be marked economically viable from public pages alone.

### Ozdingo — PROMISING ASSORTMENT / ACQUISITION PRICE GATED

Current first-party evidence establishes:
- Australia-based dropship program;
- free shipping in Australia;
- daily shipping / one-business-day dispatch target;
- 30-day Australian returns guarantee;
- Australian stock claims on product pages;
- dedicated Pet categories;
- Ozdingo Connect product-feed subscription exists (public page currently shows up-to-10-SKU subscription at A$10/month).

Public retail prices are not verified dropship acquisition prices. Ozdingo product pages invite wholesale/bulk enquiries, and the dropship program markets margin potential, but no exact per-SKU partner cost has been proven publicly.

Do not purchase a subscription, create a paid account, or contact the supplier without owner authorisation.

### Essential Dog — DIFFERENTIATED CARE/GROOMING LANE

First-party site states dropshipping is available through its website with no subscription fee and can also be accessed through Syncee and Dropshipzone. Exact dropship account prices remain account/contact gated.

Potential strategic value: differentiated grooming/care assortment rather than commodity marketplace pet hardware.

Defer ingestible/health-adjacent products in the first wave; if used, start with low-claim grooming/accessories and preserve ingredient/claim evidence.

### VetSupplyMax — BROAD WHOLESALE/DROPSHIP LANE

Public site states wholesale pet supply, direct delivery, dropshipping, fast/free delivery, and 1,000+ products across accessories, grooming, food and healthcare. Exact business economics and dropship terms need account-level validation.

Avoid medications, parasiticides and regulated/health categories in first-wave GlobalShopCo Pet.

### iPaws / PetProducts.net.au — NOT FIRST CHOICE FOR LOW-COMMITMENT PILOT

Public affiliate/wholesale program exposes wholesale pricing only after account setup and requires an initial A$500+GST order and minimum A$300+GST monthly spend for its affiliate structure. This adds commitment before unit economics are known. Keep as secondary supplier, not first pilot lane.

## Immediate commercial gates

A candidate may advance to serious Shopify DRAFT qualification only when these are known or conservatively bounded:
1. exact supplier identity and SKU;
2. owned-site dropship eligibility;
3. current stock/dispatch evidence;
4. exact acquisition/trade cost;
5. exact freight or free-delivery evidence;
6. current AU competitor price set;
7. proposed retail price;
8. positive gross/contribution screen after delivery and transaction costs;
9. returns/warranty process;
10. no unresolved material compliance/claim issue.

## Decision

Do not create ten weak Shopify records merely to fill the `/pet/` route.

The first true commercial unlock is account-level acquisition pricing from one of:
1. Southern Pet Supplies — preferred because freight/SKU evidence is already unusually strong;
2. Dropshipzone — preferred for range breadth and Shopify integration;
3. Ozdingo — preferred for free-AU-shipping structure and pet breadth;
4. Essential Dog — preferred for differentiated care/grooming.

Until exact partner costs are available, continue public exact-SKU/competitor screening and preserve candidates as HOLD rather than manufacturing margins.
