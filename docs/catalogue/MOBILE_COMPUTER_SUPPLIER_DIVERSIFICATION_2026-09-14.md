## Current preferred Mobile product shapes

1. passive aluminium/foldable laptop stands;

3. desk cable-management trays/clips that do not require mains power;
4. laptop/tablet sleeves with realistic one-unit fulfilment;

- hubs/docks with power or compatibility/support burden;
- batteries/powerbanks;
- cheap cables with near-zero differentiation;
- high-return monitors/electronics;
- promotional suppliers whose MOQ forces material stock commitment before demand proof.
