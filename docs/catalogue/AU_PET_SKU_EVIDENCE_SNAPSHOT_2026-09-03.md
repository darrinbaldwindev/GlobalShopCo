## Purpose

Advance the AU pet vertical from supplier-level evidence toward the required SKU-level commercial gate without inventing wholesale cost, retail price, or realised margin. The supplier's public catalogue exposes exact SKUs, listed product weights and stock counts, while trade pricing requires account login. Shipping is publicly documented by destination and weight band.

## Primary supplier evidence

Southern Pet Supplies publicly states that it operates a dropship service for online pet retailers, sends goods directly to customers, has no minimum for dropship orders, and requires a separate dropship account. Its customer standards require a published privacy policy, refund policy and terms, secure customer handling, a professional website, and compliance with Australian Consumer Law. Marketplace fulfilment (Amazon/eBay/Kogan and similar) requires prior written approval.

The supplier's public shipping table states that dropship rates are exclusive of GST and are calculated using weight/cubic-weight bands and destination zones. Supported postcodes may change.

## Free-delivery economics gate

For each SKU, required calculation is:

`Contribution before payment/marketing = selling price ex GST − supplier product cost − supplier dropship freight − packaging/other mandatory per-order costs`

A SKU cannot be marked launch-ready until the supplier cost and a defensible market selling-price range are captured, the destination freight exposure is tested, and returns/warranty/compliance risks are reviewed.
