# GlobalShopCo Product Blueprint

**Status:** Working commercial blueprint
**Date:** 2026-08-26
**Purpose:** Define the initial product-selection framework for the Shopify master catalogue and specialist WordPress verticals.

## Commercial model

Shopify remains the master commerce/product source. WordPress verticals merchandise subsets of that catalogue; they do not maintain duplicate product databases.

## Selection scorecard

Every launch candidate should be assessed for:

1. Demand / proven sales potential
2. Supplier reliability
3. Australian availability and compliance
4. Landed cost and gross-margin potential
5. Free-delivery viability
6. Competitive positioning
7. Return/complaint risk
8. Product durability and fulfilment simplicity
9. Fit with one or more specialist verticals

Unverified candidates remain draft and must not be represented as launch-ready.

## Initial category priorities

| Priority | Category | Initial focus | Vertical potential |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home & Garden | organisation, storage, practical household products | Home, Organisation |
| 2 | Consumer Electronics & Accessories | low-complexity accessories and everyday devices | Tech |
| 3 | Health & Beauty | non-regulated personal-care accessories | Beauty, Personal Care |
| 4 | Hobbies & Recreation | practical leisure/outdoor products | Outdoor, Hobbies |
| 5 | Baby & Kids | everyday accessories with clear supplier/compliance evidence | Baby, Kids |
| 6 | Pet | everyday pet accessories and care products | Pet |
| 7 | Fashion & Apparel | selected accessories and low-return-risk products | Fashion |
| 8 | Kitchen & Dining | practical, durable household/kitchen products | Kitchen, Home |
| 9 | Safety | non-regulated household/personal safety products | Safety |

Food/liquor, medicines, highly regulated products and other higher-risk categories should not be prioritised until compliance and supplier evidence are established.

## Specialist storefront principle

A product may belong to multiple verticals. Example: a storage product could appear in Home and Organisation. The Shopify record remains singular; WordPress storefronts filter/merchandise it by approved taxonomy.

## Free-delivery rule

Before publication, evaluate the product's selling price, supplier/landed cost, expected fulfilment cost and required margin against the GlobalShopCo free-delivery proposition. A product that cannot support the commercial model should remain draft or be rejected.

## First catalogue target

Do not optimise for raw SKU count. The first objective is a curated core catalogue of commercially defensible products, progressively expanded after validation.

## Relationship to M3

Catalogue development is parallel to the technical Shopify → WordPress → Shopify checkout vertical slice. Catalogue expansion must not block M3.

## Next research step

For each priority category, identify specific high-demand product types and candidate Australian/reliable suppliers, then score candidates against the selection criteria before recommending publication in Shopify.
