# M4 — Product Publication Gate

**Purpose:** prevent unverified catalogue candidates from being treated as launch-ready.

A Shopify product should remain **DRAFT** until the following evidence is available.

## Supplier

- [ ] Supplier identity recorded
- [ ] Supplier reliability/reputation assessed
- [ ] Product source/URL or equivalent evidence recorded
- [ ] Stock/availability mechanism understood
- [ ] Australian delivery suitability confirmed

## Product

- [ ] Product title and description verified
- [ ] Variant/SKU data verified
- [ ] Images/licensing/source suitability checked
- [ ] Safety/compliance requirements assessed
- [ ] Regulated/high-risk classification checked where relevant

## Commercial

- [ ] Landed/product cost known
- [ ] Expected Shopify selling price established
- [ ] Gross margin calculated
- [ ] Free-delivery economics tested
- [ ] Competitive positioning assessed
- [ ] Returns/refund exposure considered

## Vertical merchandising

Apply one or more controlled merchandising tags/collections without duplicating the product record. Examples include:

- Baby
- Pet
- Safety
- Home
- Travel
- Hygiene
- Storage
- Family

## Publication rule

**DRAFT** = research candidate or incomplete evidence.

**ACTIVE/published** = all applicable supplier, product, compliance, commercial and delivery gates evidenced.

No product should be promoted solely because it appears popular or visually suitable.
