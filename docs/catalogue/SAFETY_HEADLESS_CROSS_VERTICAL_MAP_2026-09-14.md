## First-wave Safety strategy

### Preferred
1. Baby proofing hardware with exact product-specific evidence.
2. Physical home-containment products with explicit installation/use boundaries.
3. Pet travel products only where crash/safety claims can be substantiated exactly.
4. Simple night-visibility products after powered-product evidence is complete.

### Defer
- video/baby monitors;
- GPS/connected trackers;
- mains-powered alarms;
- products whose customer value depends on unsupported injury-prevention guarantees;
- products carrying only ANSI/ASTM/EN evidence where AS/NZS compliance is materially required and has not been established.

## Tagging rule

A product may receive one or more of:
- `baby-safety`
- `pet-safety`
- `home-proofing`
- `home-containment`
- `night-walk-visibility`

only when the exact product use-case supports that mapping.

Never add a Safety tag merely to increase product count on `/safety/`.

## Safety launch-depth implication

The Safety headless site does not need ten unique safety-only SKUs. A coherent first assortment can legitimately reuse qualified Baby/Pet products, provided:
- each Shopify product has one canonical identity;
- each Safety mapping has an actual safety use-case;
- claims/compliance remain exact and fail-closed;
- the product independently passes owned-site economics.

A realistic first useful Safety assortment target is:
- 2–3 Baby Proofing products;
- 2–3 Home Containment products;
- 1–2 Pet Travel Safety products;
- 1–2 Night Visibility products;
with Monitoring & Alerts deferred.
