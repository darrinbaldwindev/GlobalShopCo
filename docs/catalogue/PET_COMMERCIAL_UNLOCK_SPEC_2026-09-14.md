## Decision formula for owned-site free-delivery screen

For every candidate:

`Conservative contribution = proposed delivered retail - GST/payment allowance - supplier buy cost - supplier freight/handling - returns/damage allowance - other mandatory per-order cost`

Do not use supplier RRP as market price.

A candidate may proceed to Shopify qualification draft only when:
- exact supplier/SKU identity exists;
- ordinary owned-site dropship/fulfilment is allowed;
- buy cost is authenticated;
- freight is known enough to model free delivery;
- exact current AU market price is known;
- conservative contribution is positive with a meaningful buffer;
- returns/warranty burden is understood;
- any relevant product claim/compliance evidence is adequate.

## Bundle strategy

Low-price Pet SKUs that cannot support standalone free delivery should not automatically be rejected if they can serve as:
- add-on item;
- multi-buy bundle;
- enrichment bundle;
- travel kit;
- feeding kit;
- cleanup bundle.

Bundle approval still requires exact combined landed economics.

## What not to do

- do not create supplier accounts without owner authority;
- do not contact suppliers without owner authority;
- do not infer wholesale price from public retail price;
- do not create Shopify products merely because a supplier has stock;
- do not treat eBay/Amazon permission as equivalent to owned-site permission;
- do not treat owned-site approval as marketplace approval.
