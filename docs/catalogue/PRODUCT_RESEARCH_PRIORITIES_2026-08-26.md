# GlobalShopCo — Product Research Priorities

Date: 2026-08-26

## Evidence update

Australia Post's 2026 eCommerce Report reports $82.6B in Australian online spend in 2025, with strong categories including home & garden, fashion & apparel, consumer electronics, hobbies/recreation and health & beauty. It also reports a $96 average online basket and strong value sensitivity. Pet products reached $1.4B online spend. These figures support category prioritisation but do not prove individual SKU viability.

## Initial priority logic

Prioritise products that combine:

1. strong category demand;
2. reliable supplier provenance;
3. Australian fulfilment where practical;
4. manageable delivery cost;
5. sufficient margin to support GlobalShopCo's free-delivery proposition;
6. lower return/compliance risk;
7. suitability for one or more future WordPress verticals.

## Supplier observations

Ozdingo states its dropshipping operation is Australia-based, provides free shipping in Australia, ships orders daily, and advertises product certifications. These claims require product-level verification before use as evidence for any SKU.

Dropshipzone states that retailers pay shipping costs and that Australian suppliers on its platform are expected to hold domestic inventory and offer dropship capability. Therefore, Dropshipzone should not be assumed to provide free supplier shipping; shipping must be modelled per product.

## Priority research categories

1. Home & Garden
2. Consumer Electronics & Accessories
3. Fashion & Apparel
4. Health & Beauty
5. Hobbies & Recreation
6. Pet
7. Kids & Baby
8. Kitchen & Dining

Specialist WordPress verticals such as Baby, Pet and Safety should be treated as merchandising layers over the Shopify master catalogue, not independent product databases.

## Guardrail

No product should be published or represented as eligible for free delivery until supplier, landed cost, delivery cost, retail price, margin and relevant compliance/returns risks are evidenced.
