## Decision rule once data arrives

For each candidate calculate owned-site economics using:
- proposed sale price anchored to current exact AU market evidence;
- authenticated supplier acquisition cost;
- embedded delivery cost under GlobalShopCo free-delivery policy;
- payment allowance;
- returns/damage reserve;
- explicit target contribution buffer.

Then mark only one of:
- PASS — controlled Shopify DRAFT qualification record may be created;
- HOLD — one exact blocker remains;
- REJECT — economics/permission/compliance fail.

Do not create speculative Shopify drafts before this decision.
