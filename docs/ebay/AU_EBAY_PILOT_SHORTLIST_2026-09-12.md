## Advancement rule

A Priority 1 SKU may advance from HOLD only when all of the following are evidenced:

- written eBay/marketplace approval from supplier;
- authorised trade cost;
- compatible stock/ownership fulfilment model;
- packing/seller identity compatibility;
- eBay buyer-data handling compatibility;
- safe inventory synchronisation/buffer;
- representative freight and final parcel/cubic-weight treatment;
- returns/warranty process;
- eBay fee + integration allowance;
- positive conservative free-delivery contribution.

A Priority 2 SKU must satisfy the same gate plus first establish marketplace permission and reliable fulfilment/freight evidence.

## Integration sequence

1. Prove at least 5 eligible/profitable SKUs.
2. Owner authorises eBay LINK by Omnivore pilot install/connection.
3. Configure eBay as a downstream Shopify channel; Shopify remains source of truth.
4. Publish only the approved 5–10 pilot SKUs.
5. Test listing/image/category mapping, inventory sync, order import, tracking, cancellation/stock-out protection and price rules.
6. Expand only after evidence shows reliable sync and acceptable economics.
