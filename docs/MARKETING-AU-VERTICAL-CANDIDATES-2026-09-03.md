# GlobalShopCo — AU Vertical Candidate Assessment — 2026-09-03

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** RESEARCHED / DECISION REQUIRED AFTER PRODUCT-LEVEL ECONOMICS  
**Scope:** Australian launch candidate discovery only.

## Market signal

Australia Post's 2026 eCommerce Report records $82.6b of Australian online spend in 2025, up 14% year on year. Relevant category signals include $11.6b Fashion & Apparel (+11.5%), $11.4b Home & Garden (+10.5%), $9.2b Consumer Electronics (+16%), $5b Hobbies & Recreational Goods (+17.1%), and $3.8b Health & Beauty (+15.1%). Pet products grew 15.2% year on year in the category spotlight.

These figures establish market attractiveness signals, not GlobalShopCo demand or profitability.

## Candidate set

### 1. Women's fashion / boho — PRIORITY CANDIDATE

**Why:** Strong fit with the existing GlobalShopCo direction and broad content/comparison potential.

**Risk:** highly competitive, price-led category; sizing/returns and shipping can erode contribution. Australia Post notes women's fashion remains price-led, with a significant share of shoppers waiting for sales.

**Supplier signal:** Dropshipzone currently offers Australian fashion dropshipping with no MOQ, local suppliers and product connections; Boho Australia is an Australian women's fashion wholesaler. These are discovery signals only; supplier terms, dropship eligibility and SKU economics require direct verification.

### 2. Pet products — PRIORITY CANDIDATE

**Why:** $1.4b online spend cited in Australia Post's 2026 category insights and 15.2% YoY growth; recurring/need-driven demand can support useful comparison content.

**Risk:** delivery expectations are important; bulky/low-margin products may conflict with free delivery.

### 3. Home & garden — SECONDARY CANDIDATE

**Why:** $11.4b online spend and broad product depth.

**Risk:** bulky products, freight and returns make the free-delivery requirement harder to satisfy.

### 4. Hobbies & recreational goods — SECONDARY CANDIDATE

**Why:** $5b online spend and 17.1% YoY growth; potentially strong comparison/search intent.

**Risk:** fragmented supplier base and variable product economics.

### 5. Health & beauty — SECONDARY / COMPLIANCE-SENSITIVE

**Why:** $3.8b online spend and 15.1% YoY growth.

**Risk:** product claims, regulated/health-adjacent content and trust requirements increase marketing/compliance burden.

## Current recommendation

**Do not select a final vertical yet.** The strongest immediate test candidate is women's fashion/boho because it aligns with the existing project direction, while pet products are the strongest alternative based on current category growth and potentially repeatable demand.

## Required next evidence

For each shortlisted vertical, verify 5–10 actual SKUs against:

- supplier identity and current terms;
- Australian stock/fulfilment;
- dropship availability;
- wholesale/product cost;
- customer price;
- delivery cost;
- free-delivery impact;
- returns/refunds;
- stock reliability;
- contribution after transaction and fulfilment costs.

The final selection should be made from product-level evidence, not category size alone.
