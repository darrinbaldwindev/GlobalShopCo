# GlobalShopCo — Australian Vertical Test — 2026-09-03

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** HYPOTHESIS / COMMERCIAL TEST DEFINITION

## Objective

Identify a small Australian product vertical that can be verified end-to-end before catalogue-wide expansion.

## Test structure

**Discovery → category education → product comparison → product confidence → Shopify cart/checkout**

The test should contain a limited number of products/SKUs with evidence for supplier status, AU availability, fulfilment, shipping cost, customer price, gross margin and the effect of free delivery.

## Candidate vertical characteristics

Prefer a category that:

- has clear consumer intent;
- supports useful comparison/decision content;
- has reliable Australian supply;
- can support acceptable contribution margin after free delivery;
- has manageable returns/fulfilment complexity;
- can be expanded into related products once the first journey works.

## Exclusions

Do not select a category solely because it has high retail price, high nominal margin, high commission or apparent popularity. No product is considered commercially validated until current supplier/product evidence is reconciled.

## Required evidence per SKU

1. Supplier/brand identity and current terms.
2. AU availability.
3. Wholesale/dropship status where applicable.
4. Customer price.
5. Product cost.
6. Delivery cost and free-delivery treatment.
7. Returns/refund constraints.
8. Estimated contribution after delivery and transaction costs.
9. Stock/fulfilment reliability where evidence is available.
10. Source and verification date.

## Decision gate

A candidate vertical advances only if a small product set can demonstrate a credible customer journey and acceptable unit economics. Marketing must not claim savings, best-in-category status, free delivery, availability or margin until the relevant evidence is current and verified.

## Next action

Research and compare 3–5 AU vertical candidates, then select the narrowest evidence-supported test rather than expanding the catalogue broadly.
