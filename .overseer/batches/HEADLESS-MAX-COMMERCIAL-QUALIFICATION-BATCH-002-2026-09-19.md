## Mission

Convert the strongest survivors from Batch 001 into hard commercial dispositions. Do not expand the catalogue count unless replacement of a weak row is justified by better evidence.

The desired outputs are not more names; they are:

- exact candidates worth account-level validation;
- products that can already be rejected on market/freight/stock grounds;
- supplier paths that are operationally credible;
- explicit account-gated blockers;
- a smaller 5–10 product cohort per headless site.

Shopify remains canonical. Headless sites curate and merchandise only.

## Operating loop

`FRESH SCAN → RECONCILE → PRIORITISE → EXECUTE DEEPLY → VERIFY → RECORD → REPLENISH → HANDOFF`

Evidence outranks claims. UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN. Retail price is not wholesale cost. Missing freight is not zero freight. Sold-out stock is not viable stock.

### Lane B — Market-floor / stock pruning

For each leading product, prefer exact SKU/model/GTIN market evidence.

Classify:
- `KEEP — ACCOUNT VALIDATION WORTHWHILE`
- `HOLD / UNKNOWN`
- `REJECT / NOT ECONOMIC`
- `REPLACE WITH STRONGER ROW` where a better evidence-backed product clearly exists.

Immediate negative patterns:
- commodity retail floors below ~A$50 where customer freight is material;
- bulky/cubic products with low retail price;
- sold-out brand-direct products with no strong alternate stock path;
- electrical/app products without adequate warranty/compliance/support evidence;
- fashion rows without landed wholesale economics;
- price compression where brand-direct or major retailers materially undercut the expected selling range.
