## Mission

Reduce launch uncertainty across the six specialist headless storefronts by taking the existing 300 exact-product identity pool (50 per storefront) and driving the strongest candidates as far through commercial qualification as public/current evidence safely permits.

This batch does **not** create a parallel master catalogue. Shopify remains canonical. The master catalogue batch owns category-scale breadth; this headless batch owns specialist-site curation and deep qualification of the best subsets.

## Operating loop

`FRESH SCAN → RECONCILE → PRIORITISE → EXECUTE DEEPLY → VERIFY → RECORD → REPLENISH → HANDOFF`

Evidence outranks claims. UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN. Missing freight is not zero freight. Retail price is not wholesale cost. Catalogue presence is not launch readiness.

## Architecture guardrail

Valid flow:

`Shopify canonical product/catalogue → specialist headless presentation → purchase action → Shopify cart/checkout`

No duplicate product database, pricing authority, inventory authority, order authority or checkout authority.

## Batch objective

Do not expand the headless count merely for breadth. Instead:

1. tighten each 50-product vertical to a serious 5–10 product commercial cohort;
2. run exact-product Australian market-floor checks;
3. close supplier-path evidence that applies across multiple SKUs;
4. close per-SKU input/freight/stock/packing evidence where publicly possible;
5. reject or hold products whose free-delivery economics are structurally weak;
6. identify products worthy of account-level validation;
7. promote to `OWNED-SITE QUALIFICATION CANDIDATE` only when the evidence gate is actually met;
8. preserve production state unchanged.

## Commercial qualification gate

A product cannot be promoted beyond research unless enough current evidence supports:

- exact supplier + product identity
- exact SKU/model and GTIN/barcode where exposed
- current stock or reliable stock/feed method
- wholesale/account acquisition cost
- GST basis
- exact freight/landed cost for realistic AU delivery
- delivery SLA
- direct-to-customer/dropship rights where used
- blind/neutral packing and invoice/seller identity compatibility
- image/content reuse rights
- returns/warranty/fault process
- material safety/compliance evidence where relevant
- exact Australian market price / defensible retail position
- proposed GlobalShopCo retail
- payment-fee allowance
- category-appropriate returns/damage reserve
- positive free-delivery contribution screen

Screen contribution is not net profit.

## Lane B — Exact market-floor pruning

For every leading candidate, seek current Australian exact-product pricing by SKU/model/GTIN where possible.

Classify:
- `KEEP FOR DEEPER VALIDATION` — retail headroom plausibly sufficient pending supplier inputs.
- `HOLD / UNKNOWN` — material cost/freight/stock/packing/compliance evidence unavailable.
- `REJECT / NOT ECONOMIC` — exact market floor plus known shipping/return burden makes free-delivery economics implausible.

Do not call similar-product pricing exact-product evidence.

## Lane D — Current market-floor findings to preserve

Already established in the durable issue evidence:
- Bonelk 100W GaN charger holds materially higher compact-product retail headroom than many commodity candidates.
- low-price Embellir travel mirrors/organisers have weak free-delivery headroom absent unusually low supplier cost.
- low-price Weisshorn camp mats/tables face aggressive exact-product competition and some free-delivery offers.
- heavy Artiss storage can have adequate ticket price but freight mass can destroy contribution.
- low-priced pet beds/ramps can be commercially weak because cubic freight is large relative to retail price.

Revalidate before relying on any price in a later batch.

## Lane E — Fail-closed conditions

Immediately HOLD when:
- freight is unknown and margin depends on it;
- input cost is account-gated and no defensible contribution screen can be run;
- exact product identity is unresolved;
- live stock is stale/unavailable;
- packing/invoice identity may conflict with GlobalShopCo seller identity;
- content rights are unresolved;
- delivery timing is commercially unacceptable;
- electrical/safety/compliance evidence is material and unresolved;
- fashion fit/returns risk overwhelms spread;
- market floor leaves inadequate room for free delivery and returns.
