## Mission

Take the surviving Batch 002 cohorts and reduce commercial uncertainty further by doing three things in parallel:

1. close or explicitly fail account-level / landed-economics gates;
2. replace weak commodity rows with stronger higher-value exact products where evidence supports it;
3. leave every storefront with a harder-edged, defensible shortlist and explicit next gate.

This batch must not create a second catalogue authority. Shopify remains canonical; headless sites are curated specialist presentation layers only.

## Operating loop

`FRESH SCAN → RECONCILE → PRIORITISE → EXECUTE DEEPLY → VERIFY → RECORD → REPLENISH → HANDOFF`

Evidence outranks claims. UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN. Account-gated remains ACCOUNT-LEVEL VALIDATION REQUIRED. Missing freight is not zero freight. Retail price is not supplier cost. Sold-out is not viable stock.
