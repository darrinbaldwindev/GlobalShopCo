## Mission

Drive the GlobalShopCo master catalogue toward the owner rule:

> Every commercial Shopify category should contain 50 of the best-selling / strongest commercially viable products that meet GlobalShopCo qualification criteria.

GlobalShopCo / Shopify remains the master retail catalogue and commerce authority. Headless sites curate the best subsets only.

## Operating loop

`FRESH SCAN → RECONCILE → PRIORITISE → EXECUTE DEEPLY → VERIFY → RECORD → REPLENISH → HANDOFF`

Evidence outranks claims. UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN. Missing freight is not zero freight. Public retail price is not wholesale cost. Bestseller evidence is a demand signal, not automatic commercial approval.

## Hard boundaries

No supplier contact, wholesale-account application, purchase/sample, financial commitment, paid app installation, credential change, production Shopify publication, product activation, marketplace publication, production WordPress deployment, merge, mark-ready or overall GREEN.

## Definition of an evidence-backed 50-row pool

Each row should capture, where available:

- exact supplier and product title
- exact SKU/model
- GTIN/EAN/barcode
- current demand / bestseller signal
- Australian suitability
- supplier provenance/reliability
- AU stock / fulfilment origin
- wholesale/account acquisition cost
- GST basis
- freight / landed cost
- delivery SLA
- stock/feed method
- dropship/direct-to-customer support where relevant
- blind/neutral fulfilment and packing identity where relevant
- image/content rights before publication
- returns/warranty
- compliance/safety obligations
- exact or defensible AU competitor price
- proposed GlobalShopCo retail range
- payment-fee allowance
- category-appropriate returns/damage reserve
- free-delivery contribution screen
- risk/confidence
- explicit UNKNOWNs
